How this score is calculated
Trend (35%) — is the monthly FIR count over the last 6 months rising or falling?  ·  Severity mix (25%) — how violent is the district's crime mix (Murder/Dacoity weigh far more than Theft)?  ·  Anomaly history (20%) — how many statistical spikes has this district had, from the Anomaly Alerts engine?  ·  Case backlog (20%) — what % of cases are still Open or Under Investigation?
